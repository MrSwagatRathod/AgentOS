How to play

  1. Each arrow is a winding path with a head at one end.
  2. Tap an arrow — the whole path slides out in the direction its head points, if nothing blocks the way to the edge.
  3. Tap a blocked arrow and you lose a heart (5 per level).
  4. Clear every arrow to win the level. No timer — take your time.
  5. Stuck? Use a hint — it highlights a safe arrow, its path, and its exit in red.
